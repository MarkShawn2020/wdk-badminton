## Overview

ReelVan's watermark removal feature uses advanced AI to cleanly remove platform watermarks from your AI-generated videos, leaving no traces behind.

## Supported Platforms

### OpenAI Sora

Sora videos typically include:

- Bottom-right corner watermark
- "Made with Sora" text
- OpenAI logo overlay

**Detection:** Automatic
**Typical cost:** 50 credits per 5 seconds

### Google Veo

Veo watermarks include:

- "Created with Veo" text
- Google logo
- Variable positioning

**Detection:** Automatic
**Typical cost:** 50 credits per 5 seconds

### Kling AI

Kling watermarks:

- "Kling AI" branding
- Bottom center or corner
- Sometimes animated

**Detection:** Automatic with manual refinement
**Typical cost:** 60 credits per 5 seconds

### JiMeng

JiMeng watermarks:

- Chinese and English text
- Corner positioning
- Logo overlay

**Detection:** Automatic
**Typical cost:** 50 credits per 5 seconds

## Removal Methods

### Automatic Detection

Best for standard platform watermarks:

1. Upload your video
2. Select "Remove Watermark"
3. Choose "Auto-detect"
4. ReelVan identifies and removes watermarks

**Pros:**

- Fast and easy
- Works for 95% of videos
- No manual work needed

**Cons:**

- May miss custom watermarks
- Less control over process

### Manual Selection

For custom or complex watermarks:

1. Upload your video
2. Select "Remove Watermark"
3. Choose "Manual select"
4. Draw a box around the watermark
5. Process the video

**Pros:**

- Works for any watermark
- Precise control
- Can remove multiple watermarks

**Cons:**

- Requires manual effort
- Takes slightly longer

### Batch Mode

Process multiple videos with the same watermark:

1. Upload first video
2. Configure watermark removal
3. Save as template
4. Apply template to other videos

**Perfect for:**

- Series of videos from same platform
- Consistent branding removal
- High-volume processing

## Best Practices

### Before Upload

✅ **Upload highest quality source**

- Higher resolution = better removal
- Avoid compressed versions

✅ **Check watermark location**

- Note if it moves or is animated
- Identify if it's transparent or opaque

✅ **Consider video content**

- Complex backgrounds may need manual selection
- Static watermarks are easier to remove

### During Processing

✅ **Use auto-detect first**

- Try automatic before manual
- Saves time and credits

✅ **Preview before download**

- Check removal quality
- Verify no artifacts

✅ **Adjust if needed**

- Can re-process with manual selection
- First re-process is free

### After Removal

✅ **Compare original vs. enhanced**

- Check for any remaining traces
- Verify video quality maintained

✅ **Save successful templates**

- Reuse for similar videos
- Batch process efficiently

## Technical Details

### How It Works

ReelVan uses a multi-stage approach:

1. **Detection**: AI identifies watermark location and type
2. **Analysis**: Understand background content under watermark
3. **Reconstruction**: Intelligently fill the watermark area
4. **Blending**: Seamlessly merge reconstructed area
5. **Refinement**: AI ensures natural appearance

### Quality Factors

Removal quality depends on:

- **Watermark opacity**: Transparent is harder
- **Background complexity**: Busy backgrounds are trickier
- **Watermark movement**: Static is easier than animated
- **Video resolution**: Higher is better
- **Compression**: Less compression = better results

### Limitations

What we can't remove perfectly (yet):

- Watermarks over 50% of frame
- Extremely animated/morphing watermarks
- Watermarks embedded in compressed artifacts
- DRM or encrypted watermarks

For these cases, we recommend:

- Re-exporting from source at higher quality
- Using manual selection with multiple points
- Contacting support for custom solutions

## Troubleshooting

### Watermark not fully removed

**Solutions:**

1. Try manual selection mode
2. Upload higher resolution source
3. Use "Complex watermark" option
4. Contact support for assistance

### Artifacts after removal

**Solutions:**

1. Reduce enhancement strength
2. Use "Conservative" removal mode
3. Try processing again with different settings

### Detection failed

**Solutions:**

1. Switch to manual selection
2. Ensure video is not overly compressed
3. Check if watermark is standard type
4. Contact support with sample frame

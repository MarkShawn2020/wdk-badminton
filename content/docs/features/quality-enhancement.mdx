## Enhancement Options

### Resolution Upscaling

Increase your video resolution while maintaining natural detail:

#### 1080p → 1440p (2K)

**Best for:**

- Moderate quality boost
- Faster processing
- Lower cost

**Specs:**

- Input: 1920x1080
- Output: 2560x1440
- Processing time: ~1-2 min per 10 seconds
- Cost: 50 credits per 5 seconds

#### 1080p → 4K

**Best for:**

- Maximum quality
- Professional content
- Large displays

**Specs:**

- Input: 1920x1080
- Output: 3840x2160
- Processing time: ~3-5 min per 10 seconds
- Cost: 75 credits per 5 seconds

#### 720p → 1080p

**Best for:**

- Older content
- Budget upscaling
- Quick improvements

**Specs:**

- Input: 1280x720
- Output: 1920x1080
- Processing time: ~30-60 sec per 10 seconds
- Cost: 40 credits per 5 seconds

### Noise Reduction

Remove grain, compression artifacts, and digital noise:

**Light Denoising**

- Subtle reduction
- Preserves detail

**Medium Denoising**

- Balanced approach
- Most common choice
- Good for typical AI videos

**Heavy Denoising**

- Maximum cleaning
- May soften details
- Best for very noisy sources

**Cost:** 25 credits per 5 seconds

## Best Practices

### Input Quality Matters

✅ **Do:**

- Upload highest resolution available
- Use least compressed version
- Prefer MP4 with H.264
- Ensure good lighting in original

❌ **Avoid:**

- Multiple re-encodes
- Highly compressed sources
- Very low resolution (below 480p)
- Heavily artifacted videos

### Choose Right Enhancement

**For Social Media:**

- 1440p upscale (sufficient)
- Vibrant colors
- Light sharpening

**For YouTube:**

- 4K upscale
- Natural colors
- Medium denoise

**For Professional Use:**

- 4K upscale
- Cinematic grading
- Subtle everything

### Preview Before Full Process

1. Process a 5-second clip first
2. Review the enhancement
3. Adjust settings if needed
4. Then process full video

Saves credits and ensures satisfaction.

## Troubleshooting

### Enhancement looks unnatural

**Solutions:**

- Reduce sharpening strength
- Use "Natural" color preset
- Try "Conservative" mode
- Lower denoise level

### Processing failed

**Solutions:**

- Check input file isn't corrupted
- Verify file size under 500MB
- Ensure format is supported
- Try shorter clip first

### Output quality not improved

**Solutions:**

- Input may already be high quality
- Try different enhancement combination
- Check you're viewing at full resolution
- Compare on high-quality display

## FAQs

### Can I enhance already enhanced videos?

Not recommended. Each enhancement reduces quality slightly. Best to enhance from original source.

### What's the maximum upscale?

720p → 4K (6x upscale) is maximum. Beyond this, quality degrades.

### Do you support batch processing?

Yes! Via API or by uploading multiple videos in dashboard.

### How long are enhanced videos stored?

30 days for free users, 90 days for premium users. Download anytime.

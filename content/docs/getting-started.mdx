## Quick Start Guide

Get your first AI video enhanced in under 5 minutes. This guide walks you through everything from account creation to downloading your enhanced video.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

### Sign Up Options

You can create a ReelVan account using:

- **Email & Password** - Traditional signup
- **Google** - Sign in with Google
- **GitHub** - For developers

```bash
# Visit the signup page
https://reelvan.com/signup
```

### Free Tier Benefits

New accounts automatically receive:

- 100 free credits (≈10 seconds of video)
- Access to all features
- No credit card required
- 7-day trial of premium features

## Step 2: Upload Your Video

### Supported Formats

ReelVan accepts videos from:

- **Sora** (OpenAI)
- **Veo** (Google)
- **Kling** (Kling AI)
- **JiMeng** (JiMeng AI)
- Other standard video formats (MP4, MOV, WebM)

### File Requirements

- **Max file size:** 500MB
- **Max duration:** 2 minutes
- **Recommended format:** MP4 (H.264)
- **Max resolution:** 4K

### How to Upload

1. Go to your [Dashboard](https://reelvan.com/dashboard)
2. Click **Upload Video** or drag & drop
3. Wait for upload to complete
4. Preview your video

<Callout type="info">
  **Tip:** For best results, upload the highest quality source video available. Higher quality input
  = better output.
</Callout>

## Step 3: Choose Enhancements

### Available Options

Select the enhancements you need:

#### Watermark Removal

- **Auto-detect**: Let AI find and remove watermarks
- **Manual select**: Choose watermark area yourself
- **Batch mode**: Process multiple videos with same watermark

**Cost:** ~50 credits per 5 seconds

#### Quality Enhancement

- **1080p → 1440p**: Moderate upscale
- **1080p → 4K**: Maximum quality
- **Denoise**: Remove video noise/grain
- **Color enhance**: Improve colors and contrast

**Cost:** ~75 credits per 5 seconds

#### Aspect Ratio Change

- **16:9** (YouTube)
- **9:16** (Stories)
- **1:1** (Square)
- **4:5** (Feed)

**Cost:** ~25 credits per 5 seconds

#### Custom Watermark

- Upload your logo
- Set position (corners, center)
- Adjust opacity (0-100%)

**Cost:** ~10 credits per 5 seconds

### Credit Estimation

Before processing, ReelVan shows:

- Total credits required
- Processing time estimate
- Your current balance
- Option to buy more credits if needed

## Step 4: Process Your Video

1. Review your selected enhancements
2. Click **Start Processing**
3. Monitor progress in real-time
4. Receive notification when complete

### Processing Time

Typical processing times:

| Video Length | Enhancements        | Est. Time |
| ------------ | ------------------- | --------- |
| 5 seconds    | Watermark only      | 30-60s    |
| 10 seconds   | Watermark + Quality | 2-3 min   |
| 30 seconds   | All enhancements    | 5-8 min   |
| 60 seconds   | All enhancements    | 10-15 min |

<Callout type="warning">
  **Note:** Processing times vary based on server load. Premium users get priority processing.
</Callout>

## Step 5: Download Your Video

Once processing completes:

1. Review the enhanced video in the preview player
2. Compare side-by-side with original
3. Click **Download** to save
4. Share on your preferred platform

### Download Options

- **High Quality MP4** - Best for YouTube, editing
- **Compressed MP4** - Smaller file, web-optimized
- **Original + Enhanced** - Download both versions

## Common Questions

### How long do credits last?

Credits never expire. Buy once, use anytime.

### Can I cancel processing?

Yes! Click "Cancel" during processing. Credits are refunded automatically.

### What if I'm not satisfied?

First-time users get one free re-process if not satisfied with results.

### Is there a free tier?

Yes! New accounts get 100 free credits to try all features.

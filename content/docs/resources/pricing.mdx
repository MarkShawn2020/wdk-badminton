## Operation-Based Pricing

ReelVan uses a simple, operation-based credit system:

- **Pay as you go** - Buy credits when you need them
- **Or subscribe** - Save up to 93% with monthly/yearly plans
- **Credits never expire** (PAYG only)
- **Subscription credits reset monthly** - Fresh credits every month

## How Credits Work

### Core Concepts

ReelVan pricing is based on video processing operations:

- **Standard Video**: 10 seconds, 720p 30fps (typical AI video output)
- **Enhanced Output**: 1080p 60fps
- **Minimum Unit**: 5 seconds of video
- **Credit**: ≈ $0.01 API cost

### Operations & Credit Costs

ReelVan offers three types of processing operations:

| Operation                        | Credits per 10s | Credits per 5s | Description            |
| -------------------------------- | --------------- | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| **WMR** (Watermark Removal)      | 10              | 5              | Remove watermarks only |
| **E** (Enhancement)              | 20              | 10             | Upscale to 1080p 60fps |
| **FB** (Full Bundle) - _DEFAULT_ | 30              | 15             | WMR + Enhancement      |

**Note**: Full Bundle (FB) is the default and recommended workflow. It provides both watermark removal and quality enhancement in a single operation.

## Maximizing Value

### Tips to Save Credits

1. **Choose the right operation**
   - WMR only (10 credits/10s) if you only need watermark removal
   - Full Bundle (30 credits/10s) is best value for complete enhancement

2. **Use subscription plans**
   - Save up to 93% vs PAYG
   - Monthly plans: flexible, cancel anytime
   - Yearly plans: best value, get 2 months free

3. **Process efficiently**
   - Trim unnecessary footage before processing
   - Test with short clips first (5-10s)
   - Upload highest quality source possible

4. **Plan your usage**
   - Estimate monthly needs
   - Choose appropriate subscription tier
   - Avoid running out mid-project

## FAQs

### Do credits expire?

**PAYG**: No, credits never expire.
**Subscriptions**: Credits reset monthly (don't accumulate).

### Can I get a refund?

Yes, if processing fails or you're unsatisfied (first-time users).

### Do you offer monthly plans?

Yes! We offer both PAYG (credits never expire) and monthly/yearly subscriptions (save up to 93%).

### Can I share credits with a team?

Enterprise accounts support team credit pools and shared billing.

### What happens if I run out mid-job?

You'll get a warning before processing starts. You can:

- Cancel and buy more credits
- Process partial video (what your credits allow)

### Are there hidden fees?

No. Price shown = price paid. All fees included.

### Do you charge for failed jobs?

Never. Failed jobs are auto-refunded immediately.

### Can I upgrade my package?

**PAYG**: Buy more credits anytime.
**Subscriptions**: Upgrade to higher tier anytime.

### Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, cancel anytime. No questions asked. Credits remain until end of billing period.

### What's the difference between monthly and yearly subscriptions?

**Monthly**: Pay monthly, cancel anytime, credits reset monthly.
**Yearly**: Pay once for year, get 2 months free (pay 10, get 12), best value.
